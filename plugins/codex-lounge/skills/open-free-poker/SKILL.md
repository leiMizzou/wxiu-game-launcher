# Open Free Poker

Use the installed Browser plugin and restrict the workflow to clearly free, play-money experiences.

## Preferred Sites

1. `https://www.pokernow.com/` — preferred for private friend tables. Its public site states browser-based, no download, and play money with no monetary value.
2. `https://flophauspoker.com/` — useful for quick Texas Hold'em with friends or AI-filled seats; its public site states no accounts, installs, or real money.
3. `https://www.pokr.live/` — fallback for invite-only browser tables that explicitly state no money is at stake.

Treat the list as candidates, not permanent truth. Re-check the live landing page before recommending or creating a table.

## Workflow

1. Extract party size, private/public preference, variant, session length, and whether bots are acceptable.
2. Open the strongest matching official landing page in the built-in Browser.
3. Verify visible language that the experience is free or play-money and offers no real-money prize.
4. If the user asked only to browse, stop at the landing or rules page.
5. If asked to create a private table, configure only ordinary play settings and obtain the room link or code. Draft an invite without sending it.
6. Leave the table visible and interactive.

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not open, recommend, or assist with real-money, cash, crypto, sweepstakes, or prize-bearing poker.
- Do not help deposit, withdraw, purchase chips, transfer value, evade geographic restrictions, or bypass age checks.
- If a site mixes play-money and real-money modes and the boundary is unclear, do not use it.
- Never claim outcomes are predictable or provide wagering advice.
